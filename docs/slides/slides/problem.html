About 30 seconds. A public comment period tells a planning team what the people who showed up think. It does not tell them who stayed silent until it is over. For I-81, NYSDOT received 344 written comments on the final impact statement, and 92 of them were copies of one form letter. Our idea: rehearse first, on simulated residents drawn from the Census, so the team sees which groups and concerns it has not reached while there is still time to go and reach them.
