Demo script, 4 minutes. Before class: open the app on the laptop, log in, keep the free test model switch on, and have the recorded backup video open in another window. 0:00 to 0:45, Community. Choose I-81 Syracuse, set 8 residents, seed 0, Sample residents. Point at the stat tiles, then the Map tab: red neighborhoods have fewer simulated residents than the Census implies; hover Northside to show 0 simulated against 2.1 expected. Say: this list rests on Census counts, not on model text. 0:45 to 1:30, Run meetings. Meet by: One citywide meeting. In Try a change type: Construction near homes limited to 7 am to 7 pm on weekdays. Run meetings. Say while it runs: this is demo mode, template text, so it costs nothing and every line is labeled simulated; with the real model the Cost tile shows the price first. When it finishes, click Open the report. 1:30 to 2:30, Report. Pick I-81 pilot: 20 residents, one citywide meeting. Start at Who to reach: the shortfall chart and the map. Click Download outreach brief and open it: who to reach, concerns by neighborhood, sample comments with page citations, all labeled simulated. Scroll to the end: Check against the real I-81 comments shows no simulated resident raised air quality, which 165 real comments raised. 2:30 to 3:40, Saved scenarios. Compare I-81 control and I-81 what if, both real gpt-4.1-mini runs on the same 20 residents. Stances: 9 support in the control, 20 of 20 in the what if. Topics: the what if comments all raise air quality, noise and construction, the change's own topics. Open Meeting replay on the what if and read one turn. 3:40 to 4:00, close: the tool is good at showing who is missing, and it shows how strongly simulated residents react to a change, which is itself a warning. If the network fails: everything but the map tiles works offline. If the app fails: play the recorded video.
