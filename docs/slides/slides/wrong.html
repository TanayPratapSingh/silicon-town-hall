About one minute. Where the simulation gets people wrong, and we checked this against the real comments. Across two draws of 20 residents, the meeting raised 65 percent of the topics in the real comments; a single model with no personas raised 88. No meeting comment raised air quality, though 165 real comments did. When we added one change to the plan, all 20 residents backed it, against 9 without the change. And no resident opposed the plan in any meeting, while every baseline without a meeting had one or two who did. So the simulated opinions narrow and drift toward agreement. A team should read them as a rehearsal of objections to look for, not a forecast.
