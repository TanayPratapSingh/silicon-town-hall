About one minute. What the tool is reliable for. Because every resident is a real Census record, the app can compare who showed up in the simulated town with who lives there. Our first draw had no Hispanic resident although Hispanic adults are 8.4 percent of the area, and none from Northside where the Census implies about two. That list does not depend on what the model writes, so it is the part a planning team can act on: go and reach those people in person. And across all 90 profiles we generated, none was flagged as stereotyped.
