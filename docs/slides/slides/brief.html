About 30 seconds. This is the product. After the meetings, the team downloads an outreach brief. First, who to reach in person: groups the simulated town under-represents against the Census, such as Northside and Hispanic residents in our first draw. Second, concerns by neighborhood and group, using NYSDOT's own topic codes. Third, sample comments with the pages they cite. Every line is labeled simulated.
