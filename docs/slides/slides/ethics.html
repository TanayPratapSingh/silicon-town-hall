About 30 seconds. The biggest ethical risk is misplaced trust: a planner reading fluent simulated comments might skip real outreach. So every resident, turn and comment is labeled simulated, in the app and in the data. Real commenters' names, emails, phones and addresses are removed before any text reaches a model, and an audit found none left. Profiles are written from Census facts only and reviewed; none of 90 was flagged as stereotyped. We also caught residents contradicting their own records, added their Census facts to every prompt, and the count went from 1 in 80 texts to 0 in 380. And the who to reach list makes missing groups visible instead of hiding them.
