How a team uses it

Four steps, then change the plan and run it again

1

Community

Draw residents from Census records for one area and place each in a neighborhood.

2

Policy

Upload the plan. Every page is indexed as text and as an image, so residents can cite it.

3

Meetings

A facilitator runs the meeting; residents speak, cite pages and write one comment each.

4

Report

Topics by neighborhood and group, sample comments, and who to reach in person.

Then the what if editor: describe a change, rerun the same residents, and compare stances and topics side by side.

Every resident, turn and comment in the app is labeled simulated

About 40 seconds. Walk the four steps. One: pick a community; residents are real Census microdata records, placed in a tract and a neighborhood. Two: upload the policy; we index every page as text and as a page image. Three: the meeting, which is the core: a facilitator sets an agenda, residents take turns, each turn cites pages retrieved for that resident's own situation, and each resident ends by writing a comment. Four: the report. Then the what if editor, which is what we will demo.
